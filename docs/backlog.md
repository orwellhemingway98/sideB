# Spindle backlog

Ideas kept for later (2026-11-02, after v0.7.4), best first.

1. **Sleep timer** for podcasts: stop in 15 / 30 / 60 minutes or at the end of the episode, from a long press on the
   centre button or the menu bar icon's menu.
2. **Global shortcuts**, from any app and for music too: e.g. ⌃⌥Space play/pause, ⌃⌥← / → skip.
3. **Remember the podcast volume** between launches (today it starts at 100%).
4. **Rediscovery**: a "From your library" pill suggesting a song not played in a long time, or an old episode left
   unfinished.
5. **Notch dial**: on notched MacBooks, the dial drops out of the notch instead of the menu bar icon.
6. **Housekeeping**: Apple Music folder or subscription playlists show the album instead; in lists over 500 songs,
   rows past 500 aren't highlighted.

# Spindle backlog

Ideas kept for later (2026-11-02, after v0.7.4), best first.

1. **Sleep timer** for podcasts: stop in 15 / 30 / 60 minutes or at the end of the episode, from a long press on the
   centre button or the menu bar icon's menu.
2. **Global shortcuts**, from any app and for music too: e.g. ⌃⌥Space play/pause, ⌃⌥← / → skip.
3. **Remember the podcast volume** between launches (today it starts at 100%).
4. **Rediscovery**: a "From your library" pill suggesting a song not played in a long time, or an old episode left
   unfinished.
5. **Live Activity for podcasts** (macOS 27.3 and later, in beta since 3 November 2026): a Spindle activity in the
   menu bar, and in the Dynamic Island on the OLED MacBook Pro. Minimal: the cover. Compact: cover and time left.
   Expanded: cover, title, show, progress, back 15, play/pause, forward 30. Started when a podcast plays, updated
   only on play, pause, seek, new episode or speed change. Needs a widget extension, App Intents for the buttons and
   artwork both can read; earlier systems keep the strip. No scrubbing or haptics there, so the panel stays.
   **Test first on the beta:** Spindle already reports to the system's Now Playing, so the system activity may show its
   podcasts with no work, and our own might duplicate it. Podcasts only at first; Apple's covers Apple Music. This may
   replace the notch idea below. Apple's talk: https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111467/
6. **Notch dial**: on notched MacBooks, the dial drops out of the notch instead of the menu bar icon.
7. **Housekeeping**: Apple Music folder or subscription playlists show the album instead; in lists over 500 songs,
   rows past 500 aren't highlighted.
